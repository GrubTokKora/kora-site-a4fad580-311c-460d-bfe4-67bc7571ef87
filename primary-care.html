How your first visit works

Four steps from new patient to your own physician

As a self-owned private practice, we keep becoming a patient simple: confirm your coverage, pick a time, meet your doctor, and stay connected afterwards.

With over a decade serving the Kansas community, our mission is to empower you to live life fully — establishing trust through evidence-based medicine and shared decision-making, so you understand every step.

1

Check your insurance

We accept all major health insurance plans except Medicaid and Humana. Call (913) 291-0135 before your first visit so we can verify your specific coverage.

2

Book your appointment

Schedule online through our patient portal, or call and our front desk will find you an appointment — often the same week.

3

Meet your physician

Come to 11213 Nall Ave Ste 100 in south Leawood, with free on-site parking. Your visit is one on one with a board-certified family physician who takes time to learn your history.

4

Stay connected

Use the AdvancedMD patient portal for results, refills and messages, and come back to the same physician who knows you.
