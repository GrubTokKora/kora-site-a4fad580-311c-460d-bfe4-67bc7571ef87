FAQ

Visiting JioMed

The clinic is open Monday through Friday, 8 am to 5 pm, and closed on weekends. Please call (913) 291-0135 to confirm holiday hours or to ask about appointments outside these times.

There is free on-site parking at 11213 Nall Ave Ste 100, Leawood, KS 66211. The clinic is on the ground floor with step-free access from the car park.

Both are handled through the AdvancedMD patient portal. Log in to view results, message the clinic and request refills. If you haven't set up portal access yet, call (913) 291-0135 and we'll send you an invitation.

Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department. This website and our contact form are not monitored for emergencies.
