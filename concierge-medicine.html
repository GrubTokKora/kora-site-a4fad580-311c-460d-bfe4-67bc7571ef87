Concierge Medicine

A low-cost monthly membership that adds direct access to your physician on top of the insurance-based care you already have.

Membership care

Founded on the principles of personalized physician care

JioMed was founded on the principles of personalized physician care.

We believe that the best medical care is delivered when a doctor truly understands their patient, has the time to provide the highest level of care, and the flexibility to tailor treatments to everyone. Our passion for this approach to healthcare led us to establish this independent practice.

As healthcare has grown increasingly complex, we've observed challenges for our patients in accessing timely appointments. In response, we are excited to introduce our Concierge Medicine Membership — an innovative, modern approach that establishes a more direct and meaningful connection.

How it works

Our unique hybrid model

At JioMed, we've designed a unique healthcare model that combines the benefits of a low-cost monthly membership with the reliability of insurance-based care.

Insurance benefits

Rest assured, your insurance benefits for office visits, annual physicals, lab work, imaging, and more remain intact. There are no changes to your insurance coverage in these areas.

Monthly membership

Our monthly membership offers an additional layer of service beyond what insurance covers. It grants you direct and open communication with your doctor between appointments — so you can easily reach out for any questions, concerns, or guidance outside of scheduled visits.

With our hybrid model, we strive to offer you a comprehensive healthcare experience that combines the best of both worlds — affordability and enhanced access to your trusted physician.
