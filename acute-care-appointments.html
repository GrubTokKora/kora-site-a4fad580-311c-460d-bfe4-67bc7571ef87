Acute Care Appointments

Reserved same-day appointments for sudden illness and injury — with rapid in-house testing and a physician who already knows your history.

Same-day care

Seen today, by your own physician

At JioMed, we recognize that specific health issues require urgent attention.

Through our same-day appointments, you can access timely solutions, undergo in-house testing and diagnostic evaluations, and receive personalized treatment plans to alleviate your symptoms.

We carefully analyze the symptoms by reviewing your medical history. This helps us to ensure continuity of care, minimize medication interactions, and offer timely diagnosis — the part a walk-in clinic can't do, because they're meeting you for the first time.

Self-pay pricing

$135 for an acute visit

If you're uninsured or prefer to pay directly, an acute visit is $135. That covers the physician office visit plus additional tests and labs where applicable. An EKG adds $35; X-ray is billed separately if needed.
