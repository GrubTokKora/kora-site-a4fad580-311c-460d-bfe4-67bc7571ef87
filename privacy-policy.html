Privacy Policy

How JioMed Family Medicine Clinic collects, uses and protects your information.

JioMed Family Medicine Clinic ("JioMed", "we", "us" or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of every person who visits this website or receives care at our clinic. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: August 2026

Information we collect

We collect information in two distinct contexts, and they are handled differently:

Information you give us through this website

  • Contact and appointment requests — your name, email address, phone number, insurance plan, preferred physician and the message you write.
  • Technical information — IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and pages viewed, collected through analytics tools to help us improve the site.

Protected health information

Your medical records, test results and clinical communications are protected health information (PHI) governed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and by our separate Notice of Privacy Practices, which is provided to you at your first visit and available at the clinic on request. This website's contact forms are not a secure channel for PHI — please use the patient portal for anything clinical.

How we use your information

  • To respond to appointment requests, questions and messages you send us.
  • To schedule, provide and follow up on your medical care.
  • To verify insurance coverage and process billing.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and professional obligations.
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and to measure how visitors use the site. You can refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Third-party services

We use trusted third parties to run parts of our practice, including our patient portal and scheduling provider (AdvancedMD), our partner laboratory (LabCorp), and our weight management partner (Enara Health). These providers have their own privacy policies and, where they handle PHI, are bound by business associate agreements with us. Links from this site to external websites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

How we protect your information

We maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration and destruction. This site is served over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccuracies, request deletion where we are not required to retain it, and opt out of non-essential communications at any time. For medical records specifically, your rights under HIPAA are set out in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Children's privacy

We care for patients of all ages, including children, but this website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information online from a child without a parent or guardian's involvement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting how we handle your information will be highlighted.
