Wellness Exams

Preventive care that catches problems early — annual physicals, well-child exams and age-appropriate screening for every member of the family.

Preventive care

Prevention is better than cure

At JioMed Family Care, we believe in the power of preventive care to safeguard your well-being.

Our commitment is to help you steer clear of health complications, or identify them at their earliest stages. In our primary care office, annual physicals and preventive services help to maintain our patients' overall health.

A wellness exam is also the visit where your physician gets to know you properly — your history, your family risk, what you're worried about. That context makes every visit afterwards better.

What's included

Our range of preventive care services

Annual physical examination

  • Comprehensive health assessment for all ages, including vital signs and BMI calculation
  • Well-child exams and sports physicals
  • Routine blood tests and biometric screening
  • Age-appropriate cancer screenings, vision and hearing checks
  • Immunizations and health counselling

Preventive services

  • Routine vaccinations and health screenings
  • Lifestyle and nutrition counselling
  • BMI screening and risk stratification
  • Behavioural health screening and counselling
  • Smoking, alcohol and drug abuse counselling and cessation programs
  • Nutritional guidance and chronic disease management
  • Screening for risk factors for genetic testing
  • Anticipatory guidance for age-specific health concerns
  • Preconception counselling and contraceptive management
  • STI and other risk-based screening
  • Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) for HIV
  • Sleep disorder screening

FAQ

Wellness exam questions

Most adults benefit from an annual physical. Children and adolescents follow the well-child schedule, which is more frequent in the early years. If you manage a chronic condition, your physician may recommend seeing you more often.

Most insurance plans cover one preventive wellness visit per year at no cost to you. We accept all major plans except Medicaid and Humana — call (913) 291-0135 to confirm your specific coverage before the visit.

Bring your insurance card, a photo ID, a list of current medications and supplements, and any records from other providers. It also helps to note down questions in advance — a 20-minute visit goes quickly.
