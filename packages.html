Self-Pay Packages

Transparent, up-front pricing for patients without insurance — or anyone who'd rather know the cost before they walk in.

Visit pricing

Single visits

Straightforward pricing for one-off care, with no surprise bills afterwards.

Acute Visit

$135per visit
  • Physician office visit with additional tests and labs if applicable
  • Same-day appointments for sudden illness or injury
  • In-house rapid testing available on site

Additional fees: $35 for EKG; separate fee for X-ray if needed.

Annual Physical

$175per visit
  • 20-minute visit focusing on cancer screening, risk factor assessment, and more
  • Fee includes annual physical and basic labs with LabCorp
  • Age-appropriate screening and health counselling

Ideal as a yearly baseline if you're otherwise well.

Chronic disease management bundles

For conditions that need regular review — diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, high cholesterol and more.

6-Month Bundle

$450for 6 months
  • 3 physician office visits for acute or chronic concerns
  • Discounted pricing for outside labs and in-house testing
  • Prescription refills as applicable

1-Year Bundle

$900for 12 months
  • 1 annual physical examination with basic labs from LabCorp
  • 5 physician office visits for acute or chronic concerns
  • Discounted pricing for outside labs and in-house testing
  • Prescription refills as applicable
