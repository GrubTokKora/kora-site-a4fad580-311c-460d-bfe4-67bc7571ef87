What Morpheus8 is, and what a session involves

What it does

Morpheus8 is a non-surgical treatment that combines microneedling and radiofrequency. It's used to treat the face, neck and body by improving skin laxity, smoothing the appearance of the skin, reducing the appearance of acne and surgical scars, treating cellulite and stretch marks, and regenerating the skin by stimulating the production of new collagen — which creates healthier and more youthful-appearing skin.

How long does it take to treat a body area?

After numbing the skin for about 60 minutes, the treatment may be anywhere from 30–45 minutes depending on the areas to be treated.

When will I see results? How many sessions?

Results usually start to show in the first 14 days and continue to improve over the next 3–12 months as your body builds more collagen and remodels itself. Your follow-up appointment is scheduled within 4–6 weeks after your last session. Most patients need 1–3 sessions, spaced 4–6 weeks apart, for a long-lasting result.

Results are typical and vary from patient to patient. Morpheus8 is not suitable for everyone — your physician will tell you at your consultation whether it is appropriate for you.
