In-Office Procedures

Skin biopsies, joint injections, IUD placement and more — performed in our clinic by a board-certified physician you already know.

Procedures

Handled here, not referred elsewhere

We perform a wide variety of in-office procedures at JioMed Family Care.

Being board certified in family medicine, we take it as our privilege to serve you with competency and skills. Doing these procedures in-clinic means no separate referral, no unfamiliar specialist, and no waiting weeks for something that takes twenty minutes.

What we perform

JioMed's specialized procedures

The wide range of procedures performed in our clinic include:

Dermatology procedures

Botox aesthetics, micro-needling, chemical peeling

Skin biopsy

Shave, punch and excisional biopsy, ingrown toenail removal, incision and drainage of abscess, excision of cysts, minor lipomas and skin tags

Gynaecologic procedures

Insertion and removal of IUDs, insertion and removal of Nexplanon, endometrial biopsy, Pap smear, Depo injections

Other procedures

Joint injections, ear wax removal

Insurance: we accept all major health insurance plans except Medicaid and Humana. Call (913) 291-0135 to confirm your plan before your first visit.
