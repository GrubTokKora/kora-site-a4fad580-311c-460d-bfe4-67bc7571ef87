How it works

Getting care at JioMed

Book

Schedule online through the patient portal or call (913) 291-0135. Same-day slots are held for acute concerns.

Be seen

You'll see Dr. Patel or Dr. Teeka — a physician who reviews your full history, not just today's complaint.

Test on site

Our in-house lab runs strep, flu, COVID, mono, pregnancy, A1c and ECG, with X-ray and send-outs when needed.

Follow up

Results, refills and messages come through the patient portal, with a clear plan for what happens next.
